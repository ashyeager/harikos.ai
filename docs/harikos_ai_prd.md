# HARIKOS AI product requirements

## Current product lock (September 2026)

HARIKOS is a cloud-first, closed-source SaaS for continuously verified project
state. There is no permanent Free plan. Launch plans are Core ($9/month), Pro
($29/month), Scale ($79/month), and Enterprise (custom). New eligible users may
start one 7-day Pro trial. Billing lifecycle state, persisted server-side, is the
authority for entitlement.

The V3 product requirements are maintained in
[HARIKOS_PRD_V3_FULL_MVP.md](./HARIKOS_PRD_V3_FULL_MVP.md).
Read that document for the full product definition and requirements.

For the latest dated execution audit, see [DAILY.md](../DAILY.md).
The September 9 initial-release scope follows Ash's current instruction that
payment processing may follow the login, scan, Truth, Memory, Context and MCP
acceptance loop. This does not mean the full paid SaaS MVP is complete.
